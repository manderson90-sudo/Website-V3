Privacy notice

How information is handled when you access this portfolio or contact me.

Who maintains this portfolio

Michael Anderson maintains this personal professional portfolio. For questions about information I handle directly, contact m.anderson90@icloud.com .

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages and is publicly accessible. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure its hosting service. You do not need to create an account with me to browse the website.

Information processed by GitHub is subject to its privacy arrangements. This notice covers the information I handle directly through professional enquiries and the content I publish.

Information you provide

If you contact me by email or LinkedIn, I may receive your name, contact details, professional information and the contents of your message. Please share only information relevant to your enquiry.

How I use that information

I use enquiry information to respond, discuss professional opportunities, maintain relevant correspondence and address requests about the portfolio. I do not sell enquiry information or add it to marketing lists.

Where UK data protection law applies, my purposes may be supported by legitimate interests in professional correspondence and maintaining the portfolio, or by a legal obligation where relevant.

Professional recommendations

The site includes names, professional attributions, relationships to me and quotations from professional recommendations. These are presented in the context of my career and linked to my LinkedIn profile.

Where applicable, I rely on legitimate interests in presenting professional feedback. If your recommendation appears here, you can contact me about its accuracy, presentation or removal.

Services used

The site uses GitHub Pages for hosting. Email enquiries are handled through Apple iCloud Mail, and LinkedIn provides the linked professional profile and messaging service.

Those services process information under their own privacy arrangements. Following a credential or other external link takes you to a separate website with its own privacy notice.

Cookies and technical information

I have not added advertising trackers or an analytics service to the website code. The hosting service may process technical information needed for its operation and security.

Retention and security

I keep professional correspondence for as long as it remains relevant to the enquiry or any applicable legal requirement. I review information I hold and remove it when it is no longer needed.

I take reasonable steps to protect information I handle. Hosting, account and communication providers are responsible for their own service arrangements.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the use of your personal information, and to data portability where applicable.

You can contact me to make a request about information I handle. You may also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office with a data protection concern.

Updates

I will review this notice if the site’s hosting, access or information-handling arrangements change. Reviewed 1 November 2026.
