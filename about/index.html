ABOUT ME

A little about me.

I'm a technology leader based in the North East of England, with experience across financial services, professional football, retail and hospitality. Throughout my career, I've developed a strong understanding of how organisations operate, the challenges they face and the role technology can play in improving performance.

MY JOURNEY

My career so far

I started my career in retail and hospitality, progressing into management roles where I became responsible for leading teams, managing commercial performance and improving day-to-day operations. Those early experiences shaped my approach to leadership and gave me an understanding of the challenges businesses face and the importance of getting the basics right.

My interest in technology led me into systems and process management at American Golf, where I worked on improving business operations through technology and better ways of working. In 2023, I joined Newcastle United Football Club and established its Applications & Digital function, building the team and developing the processes, governance and services needed to support the wider organisation.

In 2026, I joined Newcastle Building Society Group, moving into financial services and taking on responsibility for strategic technology supplier relationships. The role has given me the opportunity to apply my experience in commercial management, technology performance and governance within a highly regulated environment.

What I've enjoyed most throughout my career is building teams, developing people and finding ways to improve how things work. I take pride in seeing the results of that work, whether it's helping someone progress in their career, improving a service or delivering savings for the business.

HOW I WORK

My approach to leadership

I've always believed that good leadership comes from understanding people, building trust and being someone others feel comfortable approaching. I want the people I work with to feel supported, confident in their abilities and comfortable sharing their ideas.

01 / PEOPLE

People first

Developing people is one of the parts of leadership I enjoy most. I take time to understand individuals, recognise their strengths and support their development. I believe people do their best work when they feel trusted, valued and encouraged to take ownership.

02 / COLLABORATION

Building relationships

I place a lot of importance on building good relationships with the people I work with. Taking the time to understand their priorities, challenges and expectations helps me make better decisions, manage difficult conversations and work towards shared goals.

03 / IMPROVEMENT

Continuous improvement

I'm naturally curious and often find myself questioning why things are done a certain way. I enjoy understanding how processes work, identifying opportunities to improve them and finding practical solutions that make life easier for colleagues and customers.
