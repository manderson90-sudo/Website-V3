Introducing a central place for IT guidance and a conversational route to support, with practical information organised around the services colleagues used.

CONTEXT AND CHALLENGE

Making IT guidance easier to find

The project focused on helping colleagues find practical information about everyday IT tasks and available services. I identified an opportunity to bring guidance together and offer a self-service route to answers.

The approach combined a knowledge base with a chatbot, so colleagues could access information through search and questions.

MY ROLE

From proposal to implementation

I proposed the initiative and led the team responsible for its design and implementation. I also personally completed elements of the configuration.

My responsibilities included shaping the approach, coordinating delivery and ensuring the information was organised around the wider IT service catalogue.

WHAT I DID

Developing a structured knowledge base

We developed practical articles covering common IT tasks and service information. The aim was to give colleagues guidance they could follow independently.

We organised that content around service categories, giving readers a clearer way to navigate the information. A dedicated area brought useful guidance together for new starters.

WHAT I DID

Adding conversational support and step-by-step guides

We introduced a chatbot that could provide answers drawn from the knowledge base. This offered another route to the same guidance.

We also created and embedded step-by-step guides to demonstrate common processes. These supported the written articles and helped colleagues follow instructions.

OUTCOMES AND EVIDENCE

A central source of IT guidance

The delivery brought written guidance, practical walkthroughs and conversational access together in one self-service approach.

My contribution combined initiative leadership, team coordination and hands-on configuration. It also connected knowledge management with service information and new-starter support.

WHAT I LEARNED

Treating guidance as part of the service

The work showed the value of considering how people would find and use guidance alongside its technical delivery. Content structure, clear instructions and practical examples all needed attention.

Leading the implementation while completing some configuration also helped me stay close to the delivery work and the decisions the team needed to make.
