Digital compliance at American Golf

Introducing digital inspections and reporting across leisure operations, with consistent templates, practical implementation and employee training.

Systems implementation Project focus
Digital inspections Project focus
Employee training Project focus
CONTEXT AND CHALLENGE

Supporting leisure operations

I supported American Golf’s leisure and hospitality operations as the business developed its services. The work involved systems and processes used by operational teams across different locations.

I identified an opportunity to introduce digital inspections and reporting, beginning with food safety and developing the approach for other operational activities.

MY ROLE

Selecting and implementing the platform

I recommended a digital inspection platform and led its implementation. My responsibilities included developing inspection templates, configuring reporting and coordinating delivery with operational colleagues.

I also supported training so teams could use the system in their daily work and retrieve records when needed.

WHAT I DID

Developing a repeatable implementation

I developed structured templates and introduced the platform progressively as locations joined the leisure business. This created an approach that could be used for further implementations.

I coordinated with operational and property colleagues on the practical arrangements needed to make the technology accessible to the people using it.

WHAT I DID

Reporting and wider use

I configured reporting and notifications to support operational oversight. The digital records gave the responsible teams a way to review completed inspections and associated information.

The platform’s use expanded from food safety into wider inspections and operational processes, and was subsequently adopted within retail. Responsibility for day-to-day compliance remained with the operational teams.

WHAT I DID

Supporting training and access to records

I helped colleagues learn how to complete inspections and retrieve historical records. Training covered daily use as well as finding relevant information during reviews or inspections.

The implementation brought together the technology, practical working arrangements and guidance needed for adoption.

OUTCOMES AND EVIDENCE

Consistent and accessible digital records

The project introduced digital inspection templates, reporting and accessible records across leisure operations, with subsequent use within the retail business.

My contribution covered platform recommendation, configuration, implementation coordination and training. Operational colleagues remained responsible for carrying out checks and maintaining standards.

WHAT I LEARNED

Designing around the people using the system

The work reinforced the importance of understanding the physical environment and day-to-day responsibilities of operational teams. Template design, access to equipment and training all influenced how the system could be used.

I was particularly pleased to see an approach introduced for one part of the business develop into a wider operational tool.
