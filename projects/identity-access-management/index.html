Identity and access management

Leading an application integration programme, coordinating suppliers, internal teams, change planning and employee support.

CONTEXT AND CHALLENGE

Coordinating application access changes

This programme brought together application integration work with the commercial, operational and employee considerations involved in changing how people accessed systems.

The objective was to develop a more consistent approach to identity and access management while planning delivery around the needs of the organisation.

MY ROLE

Leading the programme

I developed the business case, secured investment approval and led the programme. My responsibilities included the budget, delivery plan, milestones, supplier management and stakeholder coordination.

I worked with application owners, internal specialists and external delivery resources. I also remained involved in hands-on configuration for selected integrations.

WHAT I DID

Application assessment and supplier coordination

I coordinated discussions with application owners, vendors and budget holders to understand requirements and dependencies. This included assessing integration options and their commercial implications.

I worked with suppliers on implementation arrangements, delivery responsibilities and technical coordination. The programme used a combination of internal capability and external support.

WHAT I DID

Planning changes around operations

I coordinated the delivery plan with stakeholders and followed established change-management processes. Operational requirements informed the timing and sequence of work.

I took responsibility for planning a phased approach to changes involving business-critical applications. This included coordinating stakeholders and considering how to manage issues during delivery.

WHAT I DID

Supporting colleagues and documenting responsibilities

I organised practical support and learning opportunities to help colleagues understand changes to application access. These included drop-in sessions and explanatory guidance.

The work also included service documentation covering application ownership, support responsibilities and commercial commitments. This helped connect the delivery programme with ongoing application management.

OUTCOMES AND EVIDENCE

Integration delivery and ongoing management

The programme delivered application integrations and developed the team’s capability to support further work. Its scope included technical coordination, supplier management, employee communication and documentation.

I also worked to make identity and access considerations part of future application assessment and procurement, extending the delivery approach into day-to-day operations.

WHAT I LEARNED

Considering the whole change

The work reinforced how closely technical integration, commercial decisions, stakeholder coordination and employee support are connected.

Planning delivery around operational needs and developing internal capability were important parts of my approach. Those considerations continued to matter after the initial programme ended.
