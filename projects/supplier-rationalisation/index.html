Supplier rationalisation and cost optimisation

Reviewing applications, contracts and licensing to improve renewal planning, consolidate services and make better use of existing technology.

Commercial management Project focus
Application review Project focus
Renewal planning Project focus
CONTEXT AND CHALLENGE

Reviewing technology value

This work considered how technology applications and supplier arrangements supported departmental requirements and ongoing value.

The review brought together application ownership, usage, contractual commitments and opportunities to make better use of existing services.

MY ROLE

Leading the review and commercial decisions

I led the review of applications, supplier contracts and licensing. I worked with departments, Finance and Legal on supplier negotiations, renewal decisions and opportunities for consolidation.

My responsibilities included understanding requirements, coordinating stakeholders, assessing options and planning changes around contractual and operational considerations.

WHAT I DID

Bringing portfolio information together

I introduced a centralised application portfolio to bring together ownership, supplier information and commercial responsibilities. This provided a basis for reviewing services and discussing their ongoing value.

I also introduced renewal reminders to support planning and allow time to assess options, discuss terms or coordinate a transition.

WHAT I DID

Reviewing overlapping services

I worked with stakeholders to identify applications with overlapping capabilities and assess opportunities for consolidation.

The work involved understanding what departments needed, considering the capabilities of retained services and coordinating changes with the people affected.

WHAT I DID

Making use of existing capabilities

I worked with my team to consider whether existing platforms or internally supported solutions could meet requirements.

This included developing an internal workflow for requests and a solution for an operational process. These examples formed part of a wider approach to reviewing available capabilities before committing to additional services.

WHAT I DID

Coordinating supplier and contract decisions

I led discussions with suppliers and worked with Finance and Legal on the relevant commitments. Decisions took account of requirements, available options and contractual timing.

I coordinated renewal and transition activity with departmental stakeholders, connecting the commercial review with practical delivery.

OUTCOMES AND EVIDENCE

Cost optimisation and clearer oversight

The review supported cost optimisation through supplier consolidation, licence reviews and changes to selected services.

It also established a more structured approach to application ownership, commercial information and renewal planning. My role combined negotiation, stakeholder coordination and application governance.

WHAT I LEARNED

Connecting commercial and operational decisions

The review reinforced the value of understanding departmental requirements before making a supplier or application decision. Conversations about cost needed to sit alongside discussions about functionality, support and delivery.

Bringing ownership and renewal information together also helped make those discussions part of ongoing management.
