Introducing Microsoft 365 Copilot

Leading a pilot and wider introduction of Microsoft 365 Copilot, covering evaluation, the business case, licensing and employee adoption.

Pilot and evaluationProject focus

CONTEXT AND CHALLENGE

Assessing a practical use for AI

The project explored how Microsoft 365 Copilot could help colleagues with everyday work involving information, documents and meetings. The objective was to assess practical uses and plan an introduction supported by appropriate oversight and training.

A pilot provided a way to gather experience, review engagement and consider the investment before a wider deployment.

MY ROLE

Leading the initiative

I initiated and led the pilot. My responsibilities covered stakeholder engagement, licensing, adoption planning, financial analysis and the business case for wider deployment.

Following approval, I oversaw the wider introduction and supported a member of my team to lead employee training. I also helped establish opportunities for colleagues to share practical experience and learn from one another.

WHAT I DID

Running and evaluating the pilot

I worked with colleagues from different departments to explore relevant use cases and understand where the technology could fit into their work.

Examples included summarising information, preparing documents, developing presentations and finding answers. We reviewed engagement and considered potential productivity benefits as part of the evaluation.

WHAT I DID

Preparing the business case

I developed the proposal for wider adoption, considering the investment, expected benefits, operational considerations and employee experience.

The evaluation brought together reporting and assumptions about potential time savings. I used this to support a discussion with senior stakeholders about the proposal and the conditions needed for adoption.

WHAT I DID

Supporting the wider introduction

I coordinated licensing requirements, deployment planning and stakeholder engagement. Employee training and guidance were included alongside the technical rollout.

I supported practical workshops and opportunities for colleagues to discuss use cases. Ongoing communication helped people understand how the technology could apply to their own roles.

OUTCOMES AND EVIDENCE

From evaluation to wider adoption

The initiative progressed from a pilot to a wider introduction of Microsoft 365 Copilot. My contribution covered the evaluation, business case, coordination and adoption planning.

Training, guidance and continued engagement formed part of the delivery approach. The project gave me experience of managing a technology introduction that depended on how people used it in their daily work.

WHAT I LEARNED

Making adoption part of delivery

The work reinforced the importance of considering licensing, governance, training and employee needs together. Practical examples and opportunities to ask questions helped make the discussions relevant to different roles.

Giving a colleague responsibility for training also created an opportunity for them to develop their own delivery and presentation skills.
