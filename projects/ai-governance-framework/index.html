Establishing an AI Governance Framework

Helping establish a new approach to AI governance, bringing together specialist stakeholders, developing practical processes and introducing supporting technology to manage AI use cases.

AI governance Project focus
Digital processes Project focus
Cross-functional leadership Project focus
CONTEXT AND CHALLENGE

Establishing a consistent approach to AI governance

As a regulated UK financial services group developed its approach to artificial intelligence, senior leadership commissioned the establishment of a new governance forum.

The initiative aimed to provide a consistent approach to reviewing proposed AI use cases, bringing together the expertise needed to consider their potential implications and support responsible adoption across the organisation.

An important consideration was making the governance process accessible to colleagues, ensuring there was a clear route for seeking guidance and submitting proposals.

MY ROLE

Leading the implementation

I worked closely with a colleague and specialists from across the business to develop and implement the new AI governance forum.

The initiative brought together expertise from technology, risk, information security, procurement, data protection, architecture and data.

I took responsibility for several key elements of the implementation, including designing the supporting governance framework, developing the forum's Terms of Reference and coordinating its ongoing operation.

A significant part of my involvement was translating the governance requirements into practical processes that colleagues could understand and follow.

WHAT I DID

Designing the governance framework

I helped establish the processes and responsibilities needed to support a consistent approach to reviewing proposed AI use cases.

Working with specialist stakeholders, we developed an approach to governance that considered the nature and potential impact of proposed AI use.

My contribution included developing the Terms of Reference and supporting framework, helping establish how colleagues could engage with the process and how proposals would be managed.

The approach also included arrangements for ongoing accountability, recognising the importance of reviewing AI use cases when significant changes occurred.

WHAT I DID

Introducing digital submission and review processes

I designed a central submission process that provided colleagues with a consistent route for proposing AI use cases and seeking guidance.

Working with our technical teams, I helped translate the governance requirements into a digital workflow that supported the management and review of submissions.

The aim was to make the process straightforward for colleagues while supporting appropriate governance and oversight.

This work brought together process design, stakeholder requirements and technology delivery to establish a practical approach to managing AI proposals.

WHAT I DID

Establishing a central AI inventory

I also designed a central AI inventory and its integration with the digital assessment process.

This allowed reviewed use cases to be recorded through an automated workflow, establishing a central record of AI activity.

The inventory was developed to support visibility and accountability across the organisation, providing a basis for ongoing oversight of AI use cases.

It also supported the organisation's ability to respond to enquiries about its use of AI and the governance arrangements in place.

COMMUNICATION AND ENGAGEMENT

Helping colleagues understand the new framework

Introducing a new governance process required clear communication so that colleagues understood its purpose, when to engage with it and where to find support.

I helped coordinate communications to business leaders and presented the new framework at an extended leadership event and a Lunch and Learn session.

I also redesigned the relevant intranet content to provide clearer guidance on the process and how colleagues could submit AI proposals.

Working with a colleague responsible for mandatory learning, I supported the inclusion of AI governance awareness within the organisation's learning programme.

These activities helped introduce the framework and provide colleagues with information about how to engage with it.

ONGOING GOVERNANCE

Coordinating the forum's operation

Following the implementation, I took responsibility for coordinating the forum's ongoing operation.

This involved managing the review pipeline, organising meetings, preparing agendas, coordinating specialist stakeholders and maintaining oversight of decisions and actions.

The role involved regular collaboration with colleagues across different functions, ensuring that the forum's activities were coordinated and managed consistently.

WHAT THE PROJECT DELIVERED

A new AI governance capability

The project established a new approach to AI governance, supported by a cross-functional forum, defined responsibilities and practical processes for reviewing proposed AI use cases.

Colleagues were provided with a central route for seeking guidance and submitting proposals, supported by a digital workflow and an AI inventory.

Alongside the technology and governance framework, the implementation included communications, internal guidance and learning to support awareness across the organisation.

WHAT I LEARNED

Bringing governance and technology together

This project brought together several areas of my experience, including technology governance, stakeholder management, process design and digital delivery.

I particularly enjoyed working with specialists from across the organisation and bringing their expertise together to establish a practical approach to AI governance.

The experience also gave me an opportunity to contribute to a developing area of technology management, translating strategic expectations into processes, responsibilities and supporting technology.
