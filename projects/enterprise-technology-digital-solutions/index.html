CONTEXT AND CHALLENGE

Establishing a technology function

I joined Newcastle United to establish and lead its Applications & Digital Development function. My work involved developing the team, defining its responsibilities and building relationships with colleagues across the organisation.

The role brought together application support, technology adoption, supplier relationships and opportunities to improve services. I wanted the team to understand the work of the departments it supported and take an active part in identifying useful improvements.

MY ROLE

Leading the team and its development

As Enterprise Technology & Digital Solutions Manager, I led the function and worked with senior stakeholders on its priorities, resources and direction.

I developed recruitment business cases, recruited colleagues and supported their development. I also worked on how the function should evolve as its responsibilities grew.

WHAT I DID

Developing people and departmental relationships

I introduced clearer responsibilities and encouraged team members to build relationships with the departments they supported. This meant understanding their day-to-day work, discussing upcoming needs and helping them assess technology options.

I supported colleagues through coaching, regular conversations and opportunities to take responsibility for delivery. Developing their confidence and seeing their contribution recognised were particularly rewarding parts of the role.

WHAT I DID

Application ownership and service management

I led work on an organisation-wide application portfolio, bringing together ownership, supplier information and lifecycle responsibilities. This supported conversations about application selection, ongoing value and service improvement.

The function also developed its involvement in procurement, identity and access management, documentation and technology adoption. I worked with stakeholders to make those activities part of an ongoing approach to application management.

WHAT I DID

Technology delivery and commercial decisions

I oversaw initiatives covering self-service support, Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption, identity and access management, supplier reviews and digital workflows.

I worked with Finance and other colleagues on budget oversight, investment priorities and supplier commitments. When considering new services, we reviewed departmental requirements and the capabilities of technology already available to the organisation.

OUTCOMES AND EVIDENCE

An established applications and digital team

The function developed into an established team supporting departments with enterprise applications, service improvement and digital solutions. The work brought together technical responsibilities with an understanding of business requirements.

My contribution included recruiting and developing colleagues, establishing working practices and leading delivery. The related case studies explain those projects in more detail.

WHAT I LEARNED

Giving people room to develop

This experience reinforced the value of taking time to understand the people and departments a technology team supports. Those relationships helped us make decisions and gave colleagues a clearer route to advice.

Building the team remains one of the most rewarding parts of my career. I particularly enjoyed helping colleagues develop their skills, take ownership and receive recognition for their work.
