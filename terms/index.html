Website terms

Information about this personal portfolio and the way its content is presented.

About this website

This is Michael Anderson’s personal professional portfolio. It presents my career, qualifications, selected projects and professional recommendations.

Independent portfolio

I maintain this website in a personal capacity. It is independently maintained and is not an official website of any current or former employer. References to organisations provide context for my experience.

Experience and case studies

The case studies describe my responsibilities and contributions. Details are summarised to explain the work without reproducing internal documents, financial information or operational arrangements.

The content reflects my experience at the time of the work. It should not be read as a description of an organisation’s current systems, policies or performance.

Intellectual property

Original portfolio writing and third-party materials remain subject to their respective ownership and usage rights. Describing my contribution does not claim ownership of employer-created work.

Organisation names, product names and certification badges are included for identification and professional context. Their appearance does not imply endorsement.

Recommendations

Recommendations are attributed to the people who provided them and reflect their professional feedback. Displayed quotations retain their approved wording. Further recommendations are linked through my LinkedIn profile.

External links

External websites are operated by third parties and have their own terms and privacy arrangements. Links may change or become unavailable.

Updates and enquiries

I review the portfolio as my career develops. If you have a question about content, attribution or accuracy, please contact me so I can consider it.
